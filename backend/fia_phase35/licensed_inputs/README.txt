OPTIONAL GENUINE LICENSED HISTORICAL EXPORTS

The one-shot runner works without these files and never fabricates them.
If you possess genuine timestamped exports, place them here before running:
- l2_l3.csv: timestamp,imbalance_score
- options_gamma.csv: timestamp,gamma_directional_score,skew_directional_score
- etf_flow.csv: timestamp,flow_directional_score
- volume_delta.csv: timestamp,delta_directional_score
Scores should be normalized -1..+1 by the provider/import process and must be point-in-time.

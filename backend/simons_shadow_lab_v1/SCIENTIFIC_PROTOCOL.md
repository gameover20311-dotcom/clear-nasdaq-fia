# SIMONS SHADOW LAB V1 — Scientific Protocol

## Objective

Determine whether repeatable NQ research structure exists without corrupting CLEAR NASDAQ's Forward-OOS evidence. A null result is valid.

## Sequence

1. Verify immutable source integrity.
2. Create a SHA256 research snapshot.
3. Register the full discovery experiment/search space.
4. Run interpretable discovery grids and descriptive state analysis.
5. Never auto-select a winner.
6. If a human/research review chooses a candidate, freeze its exact definition, discovery snapshot, discovery row IDs, test count and experiment hash.
7. Only forecast locks strictly later than candidate freeze may produce forward FIRE/NO_FIRE decisions.
8. Lock each candidate decision before its outcome is consulted.
9. Attach outcome later as a separate immutable resolution event.
10. Report all forward decisions, fires, abstentions and resolved fires.
11. Keep gross statistical evidence separate from assumed friction scenarios.
12. Monitor calibration, regimes, transitions, parameter robustness and decay.
13. Never auto-modify BASE_FIA.

## Multiplicity

Discovery grids report all tests and Benjamini-Hochberg q-values. BH values are a multiplicity diagnostic, not an automatic promotion gate and not proof of economic usefulness.

## Candidate statuses

- `DISCOVERY_ONLY_NOT_PROVEN`
- `FORWARD_VALIDATION_READY_NOT_PROVEN`
- `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE_NOT_PROVEN`
- `FORWARD_VALIDATING_NOT_PROVEN`
- `RESEARCH_REVIEW_ELIGIBLE_NOT_PRODUCTION_APPROVED`

V1 does not emit a bare `PROVEN` state.

## Validation minimums

The infrastructure uses 30 resolved candidate fires as the minimum for an interim forward report and 50 as the earliest point at which a candidate can become eligible for research review. These are governance thresholds, not guarantees that 30 or 50 observations prove an edge.

## Interpretation rule

Engineering PASS means the machinery behaves as designed. It does **not** mean predictive edge, profitability, or a usable trading strategy has been established.

# What is actually different in V6

None of the individual ideas—causal graphs, scenarios, ensemble agents, anomaly detection—are proprietary by themselves. The defensible engineering contribution is the **combination of precommitment, deterministic gates, temporal integrity and ablation proof** inside one forecasting protocol.

## The moat is not a prompt
A copier can reproduce agent names. To reproduce V6 behavior they must reproduce:
- the exact atomic evidence schema and evidence-genome history;
- causal precommitments and their hash chain;
- TRAIN-only regime baseline;
- chronologically filtered failure-memory;
- blind HOLDOUT governance and immutable result bindings;
- accumulated Forward-OOS prediction/autopsy history;
- ablation evidence showing which gates improve calibration/reliability.

The growing historical evidence + failure/autopsy corpus is the part that becomes harder to copy over time.

## Why the parity test is harder to game
GPT-5.6 Sol and local V6 are compared on structured causal chains, competing hypotheses and scenario distributions—not only on a similar-looking final thesis. This reduces the value of superficial wording imitation.
